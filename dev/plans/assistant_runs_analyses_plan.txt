The assistant launches analyses on small local models (2026-11-07)
==================================================================

Today
-----
Claude has the room: the catalogue, then run_analysis. Measured on small models:
 - Apple (4096 tokens): list_analyses gives 8000 characters (cut from 13000) and overflows the conversation;
   it is read-only for now.
 - run_analysis' roles and parameters are free-form objects. Apple's schema turns them into empty objects, so the
   model calls run_analysis with roles {} and can never fill them.
 - The model must read a catalogue of 14 analyses to choose one. Ollama's small models are unreliable at this.

Proposal: choose in two steps, and let the application do the casting
---------------------------------------------------------------------
A. A short catalogue. list_analyses gives names and one line each (about 37 lines); a new describe_analysis(name)
   gives one analysis' roles and parameters. All providers benefit: fewer tokens, also for Claude.
B. Roles as a flat argument. run_analysis also takes roles_text: "response=mass; group=species", which any schema can
   express; the tool parses it and says which role or column is wrong. The object form stays.
C. Quick answers first. Tools for the three questions - are_there_differences(table, measure, groups),
   what_has_impact(table, response, factors), has_it_changed(table, measure, time): typed flat arguments, the
   right test chosen by the application. Enough for most requests, and within a small model's reach.
D. Short results. A provider declares MAX_RESULT (Apple about 1500 characters). A report keeps its conclusion
   and key numbers; the whole report stays in the figure's notes.
E. Apple: READ_ONLY goes when a live test passes; see Measures. Until then an analysis request is explained
   instead of failing, and Settings says that this provider only reads.
F. Tests: fake helper for the protocol; tools for roles_text and the quick answers on a real repo.

Measures (live, by hand, not in the suite)
------------------------------------------
Five requests on the Palmer penguins demo, on Apple and on llama3.2: compare two groups; what affects a response; a
trend over time; a distribution; "fit a model". Passed: right analysis, right columns, a correct sentence.
Apple's READ_ONLY goes only at 4 of 5, without overflowing. The same measure tells how much C alone gives.

Order: A, B, C (then measure) - D - E - F. Strings are listed for translation, not translated.

Not in this plan: changing data, multi-step analyses on small models, Ollama's hardware limits.
