HINTS (Claude's review, 2026-10-17)
Who it is for. Engineers, scientists and students without a JMP/Origin/Minitab
licence: the niche is real - a free desktop app that goes from a CSV to a
fitted, documented chart and a report, with SQL underneath and Matplotlib's
quality on top. Its strongest cards: the data stays a SQLite project (open,
queryable, versionable), every result names the library function and the
method (Computed with), the NIST-checked fits, the reports, the plugin model,
and the Guided analysis for people who do not know the name of the test.

Add (highest value first)
- H-01 Reproducibility as a feature (R-03): every operation in the history
  re-runnable, and "Export as Python script" (pandas + SciPy/statsmodels +
  Matplotlib) - what a thesis, a paper or a lab notebook needs, and what
  turns a GUI user into a learner.
- H-02 Units and uncertainty: a unit per column (pint), carried into axis
  labels and fits; values +/- error propagated - engineers and physics labs.
- H-03 Templates: "same chart for the next file" - apply a figure's design
  and operations to a new table (R-14), and batch over a folder of files.
- H-04 Teaching mode: each report with a "How to read this" box (what the
  p-value, R2, Cpk mean here, in two sentences) - students first.
- H-05 Export for publication: figure size by journal column (85/180 mm),
  fonts embedded, vector PDF/SVG at once; LaTeX/Word table of the results.
- H-06 Data from instruments: a folder watcher / serial / CSV append feeds a
  live chart (Live updates exists); common lab formats (Excel with metadata
  blocks, .txt from spectrometers, HDF5).
- H-07 Collaboration: a project-to-HTML "notebook" (charts + notes + reports)
  to share without the app.
