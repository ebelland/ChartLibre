# ChartLibre — Prioritized Roadmap

**Audience:** engineers, scientists, and students who need an affordable desktop workflow from raw data to analysed, documented figures and reports.

## Scoring

- **Priority:** 1 = highest expected user value; ranked by breadth of demand, workflow impact, and competitive gap.
- **Complexity:** 1 = low; 5 = very high. This is a relative delivery estimate, not a schedule.
- **Gap:** the main competing capability that users are likely to notice. `Core` means a foundational ChartLibre capability rather than a direct competitor gap.

The order is a single recommended backlog. Related items have been merged, and completed work has been removed. “Requested most” is an informed proxy based on the stated audience and market gaps; validate the top ten with user interviews, issue tags, and usage data.

## Suggested near-term sequence

1. **Foundation for repeatable work:** ID01, ID02, ID14, ID04.
2. **Visible product differentiation:** ID03, ID05, ID06, ID07.
3. **Statistical depth with broad reach:** ID08, ID13, ID15, ID12.
4. **Specialist expansion:** imports/plugins, reliability, DOE, signal/science, ML and time series.

## Notes on consolidation

- R-07 and G-04 are one linked-selection/filtering initiative.
- H-01 and I-07 are one reproducibility initiative.
- H-03 and I-08 are one templates/batch initiative; signing and installers remain release work.
- R-04 and G-25 overlap in regression diagnostics.
- AN-07, G-05, and G-32 form one DOE/profiler/optimisation area.
- Items described as already done in the source were excluded from the active roadmap.
