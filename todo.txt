HINTS (Claude's review, 2026-10-17)
Who it is for. Engineers, scientists and students without a JMP/Origin/Minitab
licence: the niche is real - a free desktop app that goes from a CSV to a
fitted, documented chart and a report, with SQL underneath and Matplotlib's
quality on top. Its strongest cards: the data stays a SQLite project (open,
queryable, versionable), every result names the library function and the
method (Computed with), the NIST-checked fits, the reports, the plugin model,
and the Guided analysis for people who do not know the name of the test.
